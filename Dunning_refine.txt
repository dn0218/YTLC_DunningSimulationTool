带参数查询SQL: select st.SPECIAL_GROUP_TYPE_NAME, ssg.* from cc.SUBS_SPECIAL_GROUP ssg JOIN cc.SPECIAL_GROUP_TYPE st ON ssg.SPECIAL_GROUP_TYPE_ID=st.SPECIAL_GROUP_TYPE_ID  where ssg.IMEI=‘<用户IMEI>’ OR ssg.IMEI=‘<用户IMEI-1character>’   ORDER BY CREATED_DATE desc FETCH first 20 rows only;
